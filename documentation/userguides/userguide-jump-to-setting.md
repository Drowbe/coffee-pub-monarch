# Jump to Setting

**Audience:** anyone who has lost a setting in the Configure Settings window.

Finding a setting by name without hiding everything else on the page.

Foundry's own search filters the settings list: type into it and non-matching settings disappear.
That is useful when you know what you want and unhelpful when you are trying to find something near
it, or when you want to see the setting in the context of its section.

Monarch adds a second search that scrolls instead of filtering. Nothing is ever hidden.

## Using it

![Jump to setting](../assets/monarch-settings-jumpto.webp)

The box sits at the top of the settings list, labelled "Type to jump to setting...".

1. Start typing part of a setting's name.
2. The page scrolls to the first match and highlights it.
3. Use the up and down arrows beside the box to move between matches.

A status line under the box reads "Showing 1 of 3" so you know how many matches there are and where
you are in them.

## What it searches

It matches against the visible names of settings in the category you currently have open, so pick the
category first if you already know it. It does not search hints or descriptions.

## When to use which search

Use **Foundry's search**, at the top of the sidebar, when you know roughly what you want and want a
short list.

Use **jump to setting** when you want to see a setting where it actually lives, with its neighbours
still on screen. That matters when a module has several related settings and you want to change more
than one, or when you want to read the surrounding hints.

Clearing the box removes the highlight. The list never had anything hidden, so there is nothing to
restore.
